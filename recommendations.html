Grounded in Self-Concept and the Extended Self, consumers form a sense of identity through clothes matching their physical and cultural selves (Belk, 1988). Studies prove that aesthetic and psychological risks such as limited sizing and fit options are the major barriers preventing regular consumers from purchasing used clothes (Hur, 2020; Koay et al., 2022).

01

Size-inclusive racks

Theory Extended self · Perceived risk

In Malaysia, this gap is widened because Jalan Jalan Japan relies heavily on imported Japanese sizing, which runs smaller, REFASH and 2nd Street normally stock up on the standard S-L clothing and disorganized racks, and supply bottlenecks exclude plus-size shoppers from sustainable thrifting (Dsouza, 2022). To address this, retailers should introduce dedicated sections for Plus Size (XL-4XL), Petite, and Modest-Wear racks in physical outlets as well as additional filters online. In addition, solving an existing problem of size creates strong emotional trust and loyalty for customers that do not have enough options in their size range.

02

A 4-tier hangtag grading system

Secondly, large stores such as Jalan Jalan Japan may be overwhelming for first-time customers with unsorted bulk racks, whereas 2nd STREET and REFASH are often criticized for lack of cleanliness and authenticity. For this, there should be a standardized 4-tier classification of items using a hangtag system of (1) Grade A+: New with Tags, (2) Grade A: Professionally Sanitized/Almost New, (3) Grade B: Sanitized/With Minor Signs of Wear, and (4) Grade C: Upcycle-Ready, indicating the actual sanitization process used (such as high temperature steam or ozone).

03

Campus swaps & mending workshops

In addition to youth-centered activations like REFASH's campus pop-ups and 2nd STREET's presence at student hubs in locations like SS15 Subang Jaya, thrift stores can work together with university student unions and green clubs to organize Clothing Swap & Visible Mending Workshops once a month in campus facilities. Students will bring clean and unworn clothing in exchange for cashless tokens that can be used for swaps.
